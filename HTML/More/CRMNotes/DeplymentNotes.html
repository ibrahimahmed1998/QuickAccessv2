Field Deletion Checklist

Steps to follow before removing a field from a CRM solution, to avoid breaking dependent views, forms, workflows, and reports.

Run Advanced Find on the field

Before deleting a field, use Advanced Find to check whether it is referenced anywhere — views, forms, business rules, workflows, plugins, or reports.

Used → migrate before deleting Not used → safe to delete

If it is used, plan the migration

Identify the new field that will replace it and migrate existing data and references into that field before removing the old one.

Verify usage first, migrate safely, then remove.
